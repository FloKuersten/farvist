FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the framework really free?
Yes — the entire Farvist framework is MIT licensed and free forever. Pro is an optional pack of premium blocks, templates and design files built on top of it.
What does "early access" mean?
Pro is still being built in the open. Join now to lock in the founder price and receive every premium block, template and the Figma kit as they ship — at no extra cost.
Is Pro a subscription?
No. Pro is a one-time payment with lifetime updates. Buy once, own it forever.
Can I use Pro in client and commercial projects?
Yes. Build unlimited end products for yourself or clients. You just can't redistribute the Pro source as a competing component library.
Do I need Tailwind or Bootstrap?
No. Farvist is a standalone stylesheet — drop in one <link> and go. No build step required.
