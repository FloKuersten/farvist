Get started

Drop it in — 10 seconds, no build step

CDN — paste into your <head>:

<link rel="stylesheet"
  href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/farvist/dist/farvist.min.css">

Or install from npm:

npm i farvist

Three builds, same class names: farvist.min.css (~21 KB gzip, everything) · farvist-slim.min.css (~19 KB, no AI kit) · farvist-ai.min.css (~6.6 KB — just the AI-interface kit, made to drop into a site that already uses Tailwind or Bootstrap; Tailwind v3 adds the tiny farvist-ai-compat.css; see it live).
