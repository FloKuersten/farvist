Support

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing. Can't find an answer? Reach out and our team will help.

Contact support
How does the free trial work?

Your trial unlocks every feature on the selected plan for a fixed period, with no card required to begin. Upgrade at any time to keep your data and settings.

Can I change my plan later?

Plans are flexible. Move up or down whenever your needs change, and adjustments are prorated automatically on your next invoice.

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept all major cards and regional options through a secure, encrypted processor. Invoicing is available on annual team plans.

Is my data secure and private?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access and regular audits. You stay in control and can export or delete it anytime.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in a couple of clicks from your billing settings. You keep full access until the end of the current cycle, with no hidden fees.
