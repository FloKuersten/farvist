Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026 · applies to farvist.com

The short version: Farvist is a CSS framework, not a data business. We run no analytics of our own and have no accounts, forms or logins. The only personal-data processing on this site comes from the advertising described below — and you can decline it via the consent banner.

What we collect ourselves

Almost nothing. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and debugging. Logs rotate automatically and are not used to profile visitors. We set no first-party tracking cookies and run no first-party analytics.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Documentation and template pages on this site may display ads served by Google AdSense. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your IP address, device and browser information, and interactions with ads — and may use cookies or similar technologies, including for personalised advertising where you consent.

On your first visit from a region where consent is required (e.g. the EU/UK), a consent banner lets you accept or decline this processing. You can change your choice at any time via the privacy settings link the banner provides.

  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services
  • Manage your Google ad personalisation · opt out at aboutads.info/choices

Third-party requests

  • Google Fonts — pages load the Inter typeface from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com; your IP is visible to Google when the font is fetched.
  • Cloudflare — the site is served through Cloudflare's CDN, which processes request metadata to deliver and protect the site.
  • jsDelivr / npm — if you load Farvist from the CDN in your own projects, that request happens on your site, not this one.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California, and similar laws elsewhere), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. For data processed by advertising partners, use the consent banner's privacy settings. For anything else, contact us and we'll help.
